      Before you donate

      Who can donate

      • Aged between 18 and 60.
      • Weighing at least 50 kg (about 110 lb).
      • Feeling well on the day, with no fever, cold or infection.
      • At least 3 months since the last donation for men, or 4 months for women.
      • Tell the staff about recent surgery, tattoos, piercings, vaccinations or medicines. They make the final decision.

      Eat a proper meal and drink plenty of water before you donate. Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before.

      What happens at the donation

      1. Register and bring your university or national ID card.
      2. A quick health check covers weight, pulse, blood pressure and a haemoglobin test.
      3. The donation itself takes about 10 minutes.
      4. Rest for 10 to 15 minutes, then have a snack and a drink.
      5. For the rest of the day, avoid heavy lifting and hard exercise, and keep the bandage on for a few hours.
